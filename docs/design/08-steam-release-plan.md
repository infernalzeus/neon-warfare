# NEON WARFARE — Premium Steam Release Plan (monetization-free)
**Document 08 of 09 · v1.0 · 2026-06-12**

# 1. Commercial identity
- **Product:** premium single-purchase, **$14.99** (regional pricing via Steam matrix). No MTX, no DLC paywalls at launch, no ads, no account requirement. "Premium respect" is a marketing pillar, stated verbatim on the store page: *"Complete game. No timers. No microtransactions. Ever."* — this converts in the genre we're adjacent to (match-3 audiences are the most monetization-burned players on earth; their relief is our wedge).
- **Steam categories:** Strategy, Action Roguelite, Puzzle. Tags: Auto Battler, Match 3, Roguelite, Cyberpunk, Base Building, Singleplayer, Controller Support, Great Soundtrack.
- **Capsule strategy:** hero art (NOVA) over neon grid; the *animated* capsule slot and 6 screenshots must each pass the "key art" test (GDD Pillar 3). GIF-first marketing: the 5-match Singularity → battlefield EMP is the money shot.

# 2. Pre-launch funnel (wishlists are the entire ballgame)
| Beat | Timing (vs 1.0) | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Page live | −6 months (during M4) | trailer v1 from M1-slice + M4 footage; press kit |

Wishlist target for viable launch: 25k (genre median conversion ~10–15% week 1).

# 4. Launch quality bars
- Review-bomb-proofing: no always-online, no launcher, no telemetry-by-default, Linux native or Proton-platinum verified.
- Day-one: <1% crash rate from beta cohort; performance floor = Deck 60fps; accessibility set (colorblind/flash/remap) complete — these three dominate negative-review causes for indies in our comp set.
- Price-integrity promise: free content updates (roadmap doc 02 post-1.0) announced *at launch* — converts fence-sitters and seeds the "generous devs" review narrative.

# 5. Post-launch cadence
Quarterly free updates (Ghost Protocol → Twin Dive → The Forge per doc 02), each with its own mini-trailer and 1-week sale beat — the *Vampire Survivors / Hades* long-tail model. Paid expansion considered only ≥12 months post-launch and only additive (new sector + hero), never carving 1.0 content.

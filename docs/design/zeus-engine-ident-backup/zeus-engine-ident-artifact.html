Your picks are set: ENGINE Flicker, face Metal. All four thunder options are now the crackle family over the same weighty base (crack + chest + deep sub + roll) — what changes is how loud and dense the electric ticks sit. The previous crackle is dialled back; ticks also taper as they go so they don't dominate the tail.

  • FaintCrackle is a whisper under the strike — 8 quiet low ticks. The bolt and the bass carry it.
  • BalancedDefault. 10 ticks at a moderate level across the first ~0.5 s — audible electric texture, not the star.
  • Pops6 fewer-but-lower ticks spread wider — distinct snaps rather than a fizz.
  • Rolling15 very quiet ticks spread over ~1.3 s so the whole tail keeps faintly snapping as it rolls off.

Coming to Google Play

NEON WARFARE

Match-3 meets neon lane warfare.

A single-player strategy game that fuses a match-3 board with real-time lane combat. Mine gems, deploy troops, seize the pylons, break the core.

How it plays

  1. 1Match gems on the board — each colour mines one of five resources
  2. 2Spend resources to deploy troops — four ground lanes and the air lane
  3. 3Push the frontline and hold the pylons — they power you up
  4. 4Trigger a SURGE, then break the enemy core — before the timer runs out

What's in it

20-level campaign

Three acts, a rising curve, a director that adapts to you.

Nine unit types

Ground, air and siege in a rock-paper-scissors triangle.

Crossover strikes

Big board matches fire lasers, orbital hits, EMP and shockwaves into the fight.

Ghost ladder

Every match is recorded and replayed as your opponent. Climb a daily skill-rated board.

Eight themes

The whole game re-skins — try the buttons under the screen.

Plays offline

The campaign needs no connection. The leaderboard is optional.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No third-party tracking.

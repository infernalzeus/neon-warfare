NEON WARFARE

Match-3 meets neon lane warfare.

A single-player strategy game that fuses a match-3 board with real-time lane combat. Mine gems, deploy troops, seize the pylons, break the core.

How it plays

  1. 1Match gems on the board — each colour mines one of five resources
  2. 2Spend resources to deploy troops — four ground lanes and the air lane
  3. 3Push the frontline and capture the pylons — hold them to power up
  4. 4Trigger a SURGE, then destroy the enemy core — before the timer runs out

Eight battlefields, one per theme

What's in it

20-level campaign

Three acts with a rising difficulty curve and a director that adapts to you.

Nine unit types

Ground, air and siege, tied together by a rock-paper-scissors class triangle.

Crossover strikes

Big matches on the board fire lasers, orbital hits, EMP and shockwaves into the battlefield.

Ghost ladder

Every match is recorded as a “ghost” and replayed as your opponent. Climb a daily skill-rated leaderboard against other players' runs.

Themes & cosmetics

Eight colour themes and unlockable board skins — bought only with tokens you earn by playing.

Plays offline

The whole campaign runs with no connection. The online leaderboard is optional.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No third-party tracking.
